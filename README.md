### 💼 Payroll Forensic Analysis — Detecting Pay Anomalies Using SQL

This analysis performs a forensic-style audit on departmental payroll data to identify potential irregularities, corruption indicators, or talent diversity gaps. It calculates the **Coefficient of Variation (COF)** — the ratio of standard deviation to mean salary — for each employee and job title, quantifies pay volatility across departments, and then isolates outliers above statistical quartiles (using percentile analysis). By cross-referencing these anomalies with behavioral metrics like **vacation hours**, it highlights employees or departments with unusually erratic pay trends, suggesting areas for deeper HR or compliance investigation. The result ranks the **top three most suspicious employees per role**, turning SQL into a forensic data intelligence tool.
